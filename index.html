Privacy Policy for Digital Clock Voice

Last Updated: October 8, 2026
Summary: Digital Clock Voice respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data. Voice commands are processed locally on your device, and all alarms, settings, and timers remain strictly on your phone.

1. Information Collection and Use

Digital Clock Voice is designed as a private, offline-first digital clock utility. We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device hardware IDs.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

The application requests only permissions strictly necessary for clock, alarm, and hands-free features:

  • Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely to enable voice recognition when you tap the microphone or speak hands-free commands (such as "What is time?", "Set timer for 5 minutes", or "Start stopwatch"). Audio streams are processed in transient memory directly on your device. Audio is never saved, recorded to disk, uploaded to cloud databases, or shared with third parties.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to deliver tactile alert feedback when an alarm rings or a countdown timer completes.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Used solely when the user enables the "Keep Screen Awake" bedside clock setting, allowing the display to stay illuminated while docked.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to notify you of elapsed timers or active alarms when the application is running in the background.

3. Data Storage

All user preferences—including color themes, custom typography, 12/24 hour display format, periodic voice announcement intervals, and created alarms—are saved locally on your device via Android's local SharedPreferences. No remote database or cloud synchronizer is used.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Digital Clock Voice does not contain third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, behavioral profilers, or telemetry trackers. Voice synthesis utilizes the standard Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine installed on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in this document and accessible directly within the application settings.
